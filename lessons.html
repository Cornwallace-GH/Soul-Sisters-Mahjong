Reserve Your Seat

From first tile to "Mahjong!"

  1. Meet the tiles. Suits, winds, dragons, flowers, and jokers.
  2. Read the card. How the National Mah Jongg League card and its hands work.
  3. Set up and pass. Build the walls and play the Charleston.
  4. Play the game. Picking, discarding, calling, exposing, and declaring Mahjong.
  5. Leave confident. Guided play so you're ready for your next table.

Good to know

Do I need any experience?

None at all. The lesson starts from the very beginning, and nobody is expected to memorize the card on day one.

What should I bring?

Just yourself. Tiles, racks, and snacks are provided. If you already own the current NMJL card, bring it along.

Can I come with a friend?

Absolutely. Book seats together, or gather three friends and fill the whole table.
