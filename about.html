Our Story

The story behind the name.

Mahjong helped me rediscover connection. A lifelong friendship gave that connection a name.

Sometimes the most meaningful things in life begin when we least expect them.

Mahjong became an unexpected source of comfort, joy, and connection for me. It challenged my mind, gave me something to look forward to, and helped me build new friendships in a community I had only recently begun calling home.

For more than 20 years, my best friend and I have shared a bond that goes beyond friendship. We've always called each other soul sisters, and we often daydreamed about what we might someday create together under that name.

When she visited in February 2026, I taught her the game that had become such an important part of my life. In that moment, I knew the name was perfect: Soul Sisters Mahjong.

Photo of Mindi

The name had been there all along. It simply took finding the right passion to give it purpose.

Today, Soul Sisters Mahjong is about bringing beauty and intention to the mahjong table and honoring the people who gather around it, through thoughtfully designed racks and essentials, curated discoveries, and welcoming lessons. Whether you're new to the game or have played for years, there's room for connection.

Because sometimes the people we meet around the mahjong table become so much more than the people we play with.

— Mindi Potts, Founder
