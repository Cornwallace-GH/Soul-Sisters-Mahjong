The story behind the name.

Mahjong helped me rediscover connection. A lifelong friendship gave that connection a name.

For more than 20 years, my best friend and I have called each other soul sisters. When she visited in February 2026, I taught her the game that had become such a joy in my life, and the name finally found its purpose.

Today, Soul Sisters Mahjong brings beauty and intention to the table through original racks, curated finds, and welcoming lessons. Whether you're new to the game or have played for years, there's a seat for you.

— Mindi Potts, Founder
