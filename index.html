What You'll Learn

From first tile to "Mahjong!"

  1. Meet the tiles. Suits, honors, flowers, and jokers.
  2. Read the card. The National Mah Jongg League card, hand patterns, and key rules.
  3. Set up and pass. Build the walls and play the Charleston.
  4. Play the game. Picking, discarding, calling, exposing, and declaring Mahjong.
  5. Leave confident. Guided exercises and real gameplay.

How the Three Hours Flow

One step at a time.

Hour one

Welcome and introductions, an overview of American Mahjong, then meeting the tiles: Bams, Craks, Dots, Winds, Dragons, Flowers, and Jokers.

Hour two

Reading the NMJL card, then hands-on practice matching sample hands to lines on the card.

Hour three

Setting up the table, the Charleston, and guided play: picking, discarding, calling, exposing, and declaring Mahjong.

You leave with

Real confidence to sit down at a table, plus a few new friends.

About Soul Sisters

Mahjong. Sisterhood. Connection.

How it began

Soul Sisters grew from a friendship between two women who shared the same last name, went to the same school, and lived in the same neighborhood, yet didn't meet until later in life.

They became instant soul sisters, and their friendship has lasted more than 25 years. That unexpected connection is the heart of everything we do: wonderful relationships can begin at a Mahjong table.
