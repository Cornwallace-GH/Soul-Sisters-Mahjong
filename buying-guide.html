Choosing your mahjong set.

A good set is one you love to play with. Here's what to look for, whether it's your first set or your forever set.

What to look for

Material

Durability, weight, finish, and how the tiles feel in your hand. Look for consistency from tile to tile.

Finish and detail

Crisp engraving or printing, even paint, clean edges, and no sloppy fill.

Readability

Can you tell bams, craks, dots, winds, dragons, flowers, and jokers apart at a glance? Contrast matters.

Size and handling

Mostly personal preference. Check the dimensions, how they fit your racks, and how easily they travel.

Good, Better, Best

Good

  • Clear symbols
  • Consistent tiles
  • Reliable construction
  • Comfortable size
  • Practical starter value

Better

  • More refined finish
  • Stronger readability
  • More consistent decoration
  • Comfortable hand-feel

Best

  • Premium craftsmanship
  • Exceptional legibility
  • Consistent detailing
  • Materials and size that fit you

A higher price doesn't always mean a better set. Choose the one that feels right at your table.
